    01Operator profile

    I build the game, then the servers that keep it alive.

    I started out writing gameplay code, chasing the perfect recoil curve and the frame where a hit feels right. Then I followed the packets: how does that hit feel just as good at 120 ms of latency, with a hundred thousand players online and someone trying to cheat?

    Today I fight on both sides of the wire: gameplay and engine work in Unity and Unreal, and the backend that makes it multiplayer — dedicated servers, netcode, matchmaking, progression, economies, and the telemetry that tells us whether players are having fun.
